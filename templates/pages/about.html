About {{ SITE_NAME }}

{{ SITE_NAME }} is an AI-powered background remover built for anyone who needs clean, professional product photos, portraits or graphics in seconds — no design software, no manual masking, no waiting on an editor.

Upload any JPG, PNG or WEBP photo and our on-device AI model detects the foreground and cuts it out automatically. You can keep the transparent result, drop in a solid colour, or place your subject on a completely new background image — all from the same page.

Who uses it

  • Designers and marketers building banners, ads and social posts
  • Photographers delivering quick client previews
  • Anyone who just needs one image cleaned up, fast
