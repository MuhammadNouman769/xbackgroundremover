1. What we collect

When you use {{ SITE_NAME }} we may collect: the images you upload for background removal, account details you provide when you sign up (username, email, password — stored hashed), and basic technical data (IP address, browser type) for security and abuse prevention.

2. How we use your images

Uploaded images are processed automatically by our AI model purely to remove or replace the background and return your result. Images are not viewed by a human, sold, or used to train third-party advertising profiles.

3. How long we keep data

Guest uploads are kept only long enough to show you the result and let you download it. If you're logged in, your processed images are kept in your account history until you delete them or close your account.

4. Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and to protect forms from cross-site attacks (CSRF). If Google Analytics or Google AdSense is enabled on this deployment, those services may set their own cookies — see Google's own privacy policy for details on how they process that data.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data or images. We only share data with service providers where necessary to run the Service (for example, hosting and email delivery), and when required by law.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data and images we hold about you at any time by contacting us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us.
